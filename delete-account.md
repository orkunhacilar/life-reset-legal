# Delete Your Life Reset Account

You can delete your Life Reset account and its synchronized data at any time.

## Delete from the app

1. Open **Life Reset** and go to the **Profile** tab.
2. Open **Your Data**.
3. Tap **Delete My Account** and confirm.

For security, Apple or Google may require you to sign in again before deletion. Account deletion removes your Firebase authentication account and synchronized Life Reset data. Data stored only on your device can be erased separately in the app or by uninstalling Life Reset.

## Request deletion without the app

Email [orkun.hacilar@gmail.com](mailto:orkun.hacilar@gmail.com?subject=Life%20Reset%20account%20deletion%20request) from the address associated with your Life Reset account. Use the subject **Life Reset account deletion request**. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing the request.

We will delete the account and synchronized habit, reset, check-in, progress, preference and profile data associated with it. We aim to complete verified requests within 30 days.

Purchase and transaction records controlled by Apple, Google or RevenueCat may be retained where required for accounting, fraud prevention, legal compliance or entitlement restoration. Their retention periods are governed by their own policies. Backup copies may remain temporarily until they are overwritten through normal backup rotation.
